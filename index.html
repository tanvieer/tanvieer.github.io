Facemosque – Prayer Times

Facemosque

com.mosqueconnect.app

Facemosque puts your own mosque in your pocket. Instead of calculated times, it shows the prayer and iqamah times your mosque actually publishes, and lets the mosque reach its community with announcements.

Features
  • Your mosque's prayer and iqamah times, with a countdown to the next prayer.
  • Prayer reminders and optional mosque announcements via push notifications.
  • Mosque directory sorted by distance from you.
  • Qibla direction, digital tasbih and the 99 Names of Allah.
  • Home-screen widget with today's times.
Privacy by Design
  • No accounts: never asks for a name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, and no data sold.
  • Analytics and announcements are off until the user turns them on.
  • Location is used only while the app is open, to sort mosques by distance.
Tech
  • React Native with Expo, native home-screen widgets for Android and iOS.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and opt-in Analytics.

Facemosque Adhan – ESP32 / Raspberry Pi / Alexa

Calls the adhan at every prayer time, at home, using the mosque's own times from the Facemosque API. It started as an always-on Raspberry Pi playing the adhan through an Echo Dot over Bluetooth, and grew into four pieces that can run side by side.

Raspberry Pi (in production)
  • Fetches and caches prayer times, and falls back to a bundled year of times with no internet.
  • Streams the adhan recording to an Echo Dot over Bluetooth A2DP.
  • Handles Friday jummah, and survives reboots and power cuts unattended.
  • adhanctl CLI: doctor, pair, next, show, play, stop, bundle.
ESP32
  • Does the Pi's whole job on a single €9 ESP32 board, verified on hardware.
  • Emulates a Philips Hue device so it can be stopped by voice: "Alexa, turn off adhan".
  • Set up from a phone (WiFi and API key), no laptop needed.
Alexa Skill
  • Answers spoken questions: next prayer, today's times, jummah, iqamah.
  • Works fully offline from a bundled year of times, so no API key sits in the cloud.

HabitVault – Block Reels & Reclaim Focus

HabitVault

com.habit_vault

Reclaim your time from the endless scroll. HabitVault blocks the short-form video feeds designed to hijack your attention — Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels — and shows you exactly how much time they were costing you.

The moment a Reels or Shorts feed loads, it's blocked. You keep messaging, posting, and using the rest of the app normally. Only the time-sink feeds get cut off.

How It Works
  • Runs entirely on your device using a private, on-device filter.
  • Detects and blocks short-form video feeds the instant they appear.
  • No accounts, no browsing history sent anywhere — fully private by design.
  • Your normal connection is never interrupted; only the short-video feeds are filtered.
See Where Your Time Goes
  • Time wasted today, front and center.
  • A breakdown by app so you see your biggest pull.
  • Weekly trend so you can watch the habit shrink over time.
Features
  • Blocks Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts & Facebook Reels.
  • Works in the background — set it once and forget it.
  • Tracks time wasted before each block, automatically.
  • Daily, per-app, and weekly insights.
  • Lightweight and battery-conscious.
Personal-use app. Available only on Google Play internal testing — not publicly available.
MultiCards – Multiplayer Card Games

MultiCards

cardora.multicards.app

MultiCards is a multiplayer card game hub: seven games in one app, played live against friends or other players online. It started as a single game, Don't Draw That, and grew into a shared platform with one account, one friends list and one set of leaderboards across every game.

Games
  • Don't Draw That — draw a bomb and you're out; 21 card types, Defuse and Nope wars.
  • Hearts — take the fewest points, or shoot the moon.
  • Poker — no-limit Texas Hold'em tournament.
  • Twenty-Nine — bid, hide the trump, take the points.
  • Callbreak — call your tricks, then take them.
  • Colour Match — match the colour, dump your hand.
  • Wortblitz — a quick German vocabulary quiz for study groups.
Multiplayer & Social
  • Private rooms with a 6-digit invite code, or public tables via Quick Match.
  • Friends, invites, per-game leaderboards and match history.
  • Resume a game in progress after a disconnect.
  • Runs on Android and in the browser from the same codebase.
Tech
  • React Native with Expo Router and TypeScript for Android and web.
  • Supabase: Postgres, Auth, Realtime and Edge Functions.
  • A deterministic, server-validated rule engine for each game, so hidden cards stay hidden and no client can cheat.
